## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I scan a business card?
Open the app → tap **Scan** in the bottom tab bar → tap **Take Photo** to use the camera, or **Upload Image** to pick a photo from your library. ReachDog auto-detects the card edges, extracts the contact details, and takes you to a review screen where you can edit anything before saving.

### The OCR missed a field. Can I fix it?
Yes. On the **Review Contact** screen every field is editable. Tap the field, type the correction, and tap **Save Lead**.

### Does ReachDog work without an internet connection?
**Mostly yes.** Scanning, OCR, saving leads, editing, tagging, browsing, and CSV export all work offline. The only feature that requires internet is the AI-generated follow-up emails.

### Where is my data stored?
All your leads, notes, card images, and templates are stored **on your iPhone only**, using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it. We don't keep copies on a server.

### How do I export my leads?
Go to **Settings → Data → Export CSV**. ReachDog will share a CSV file that you can save to Files, email, or open in Excel / Numbers / Google Sheets.

### How does the AI email draft work?
Open any lead → tap **Generate Follow-Up** → choose a tone (Friendly, Formal, Confident, etc.) and length → ReachDog generates two variations you can edit, save as a template, or open in Mail / Gmail / Outlook / Messages.

### Can I change the email app that opens?
Yes. **Settings → Email** lets you pick your default: Apple Mail, Gmail, Outlook, or Messages.

### How do I delete a lead?
Swipe left on any row in the **Leads** tab and tap **Delete**.

### How do I delete my account?
Email us at [hamud313@gmail.com](mailto:hamud313@gmail.com) from the address you signed in with. We'll remove your authentication record within 2 business days. All your leads are on your device — delete the app to remove them.

### Which iPhones are supported?
ReachDog supports **iPhones running iOS 17 or newer**. iPhones with iOS 26+ get an upgraded OCR engine for even better card recognition.

### Do you collect analytics or track me?
**No.** We don't use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for the full picture.

## Troubleshooting

### Scan button is greyed out
Your device doesn't support the document scanner (usually only an issue on simulators or very old devices). Use **Upload Image** to scan a photo from your library instead.

### "Camera access denied"
Go to **iOS Settings → ReachDog → Camera** and enable access, then restart the app.

### "Photos access denied"
Go to **iOS Settings → ReachDog → Photos** and set access to **All Photos** or **Selected Photos**, then restart the app.

### Sign-in fails
Make sure you have a working internet connection. If using Google Sign-In, confirm your Google account is active. Try signing out and back in. If the problem persists, email us.

### AI follow-up says "couldn't generate email"
This usually means a temporary network issue. Wait a moment and try again. If it keeps happening, check your internet connection or email us.

### App crashes on launch
Force-quit the app (swipe up from the app switcher), then reopen. If it keeps crashing, try restarting your iPhone. If the crashing persists, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version — we'll diagnose it from there.
